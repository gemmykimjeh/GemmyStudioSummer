## FORMULAS & CALCULATIONS
[calc-00006] helpful=2 harmful=0 :: In multi-step calculations, show intermediate values and carry units through each step; round only at the final answer, not mid-calculation.
[calc-00007] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: Guard computations against error and edge cases: handle division-by-zero, empty/None, and out-of-range inputs, and flag missing or invalid data before finalizing the result.

[calc-00011] helpful=0 harmful=0 :: When calculating sample sizes for known population sizes (N), apply the Finite Population Correction (FPC) factor: n_adj = (n * N) / (n + N - 1), where n is the sample size for an infinite population.

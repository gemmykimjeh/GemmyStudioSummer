## Why ACE Qualifies as an Accepted Baseline

Three independent groups, three unrelated domains, all treat ACE as the reference point rather than building their own memory-evolution scheme:

| Paper | Domain | Role of ACE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meta-Harness (arXiv:2603.28052) | Text classification, math retrieval, agentic coding | Hand-designed SOTA baseline to beat |
| AHE (arXiv:2604.25850) | Terminal-Bench 2, SWE-bench | Self-evolving baseline to beat |

## 1. Meta-Harness (Stanford, arXiv:2603.28052):

Reference count :83 (released in March, 2026)

Framed as **"the current state of the art in context construction"** among human-designed harnesses — categorized separately from program-search optimizers (GEPA, OpenEvolve, TTT-Discover). Also used to seed Meta-Harness's own search population.

The protocol: an LLM (GPT-OSS-120B, held fixed across every method) receives labeled training examples **one at a time**, updates whatever memory/context mechanism the harness provides, and is periodically evaluated on a held-out test set. Every row in the table — Few-Shot, ACE, Meta-Harness — is the *same frozen base model* wrapped in a *different harness*. The comparison isolates harness quality, not model quality.

**In-domain datasets:**

- **LawBench** — predicts criminal charges from case descriptions (215 classes — very hard, huge label space)
- **Symptom2Disease (S2D)** — predicts diseases from symptom descriptions (22 classes)
- **USPTO-50k** — predicts precursor reactants from product molecules (180 classes)

**In-domain (LawBench/S2D/USPTO):**

| Harness | Avg Acc | Ctx (K) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Few-Shot (32) | 35.4 | 7.9 |
| **ACE** | **40.9** | **50.8** |
| Meta-Harness | 48.6 | 11.4 |
- **Avg Acc** — mean classification accuracy across the three datasets (in-domain table) or across all nine unseen datasets (OOD table).
- **Ctx (K)** — average *additional* context tokens (in thousands) the harness injects beyond the bare classification prompt. This is the "memory tax" — how much extra text the harness has to carry to get its accuracy. Lower is cheaper

**OOD (9 unseen datasets):**

| Harness | Avg Acc | Ctx (K) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Few-shot (32) | 69.6 | 5.2 |
| **ACE** | **70.2** | **11.7** |
| Meta-Harness | 73.1 | 7.3 |

Strongest hand-designed baseline in-domain and OOD — beats every few-shot variant, only loses to a purpose-built search method.

ACE outperforms every zero-shot and few-shot configuration on out-of-distribution generalization, and is competitive-to-slightly-ahead in-domain — while being categorized by an independent, well-cited paper as representative of the state of the art in *hand-designed* context construction.

## 2. AHE (Fudan/PKU/Qiji Zhifeng, arXiv:2604.25850)

Reference count: 24 

Used as one of two self-evolving baselines on Terminal-Bench 2, seeded identically to AHE for a controlled comparison.

**Terminal-Bench 2 (89 tasks):**

| Method | All | Hard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NexAU0 (seed) | 69.7% | 51.7% |
| **ACE** | **68.9%** | **48.9%** |
| AHE | 77.0% | 53.3% |

**SWE-bench-verified transfer:**

| Method | Success | Tokens (K) | Succ/Mtok |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ACE** | **74.6%** | **679** | **1.10** |
| AHE | 75.6% | 461 | 1.64 |

AHE's diagnosis: ACE's playbook is prompt-only, so on a new task surface "that text adds cost without reshaping the underlying policy" — a "layer mismatch," not a rejection of the method.

Included as one of only two legitimate self-evolving baselines on a competitive benchmark — inclusion itself signals status, even where this paper's ablation argues limits.

## 3. My Opinion

Above analysis indicates that harness self-improvement frameworks such as Meta-harness and AHE  try to optimize harness performance for given benchmark set( text classification and terminal-bench) , one of the very few human-engineered harness they referred to is ACE, which only handles contexts. Those show that ACE is still one of the most reliable context-engineering papers. One important point that we do not still know how can ACE be effective and strong reference point in web-search and long-context documentation tasks.

- Harness engineering을 해야함. Harness의 구성요소는 무엇인가? ACE의 제약사항 (not only limitation, but also constraints for structure itself)
- ACE는 context, playbook을 표면적으로 건드리는데, harness에는 그것 외에도 다른 개념, 축들이 존재함. ACE들이 이것들까지 자연스레 커버하는지 최소한 정성적인 평가가 필요.
- Meta-harness, AHE를 사용하지 못하는 이유를 조사해보고, 그러한 제약이 ACE로 harness engineering을 하면서 또 생길 문제이진 않을지 확인해보기.

ACE가 커버하지 못한다 → 새로운 baseline 조사 필요

ACE가 커버하고 Meta-harness, AHE의 문제가 재발되지 않는다 → 좋은 baseline

- We need to do harness engineering. What are the components of a harness? The constraints of ACE (not only limitations, but also constraints for the structure itself).
- ACE only touches context and playbook on the surface, but a harness has other concepts and axes beyond those. We need at least a qualitative evaluation of whether ACE naturally covers these as well.
- Investigate the reasons why meta-harness and AHE can't be used, and check whether those same constraints might resurface as problems when doing harness engineering with ACE.

ACE doesn't cover it → need to research a new baseline

ACE covers it and the meta-harness/AHE problems don't recur → good baseline
